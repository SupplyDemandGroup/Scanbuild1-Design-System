## Field 1 — Brand description (required)

ScanBuild1 — a building inspection and spatial intelligence company for Australian property developers, insurers and building owners. We capture physical buildings (Matterport, LiDAR, 3D scanning) and turn them into structured digital evidence: digital twins, evidence-based inspection reports, and Towers Passport — an end-to-end digital tower service for multi-level residential developers covering sales, construction tracking and handover in one spatial story. ## Field 2 — Style notes (optional)

Dark-first technical precision. Palette: deep navy #011A40 (primary canvas), cobalt #004AAD (brand blue on light), signal yellow #F2CB07 (scan lines, key stats, CTAs, footer bars — never a background fill), sky #6CA7F9 and ice #A8CBFD (data/mesh), mist #F4F7F8 (light pages), azure #253238 (headings on light), slate #5B7B8A (secondary text on light). Typography: Chakra Petch for display/headlines (500–700, uppercase tracked for labels), IBM Plex Sans for body (300–500), IBM Plex Mono for tracked uppercase stage labels, data and measurements. Signature devices: horizontal yellow scan line across building imagery; faint blue wireframe mesh/point-cloud overlay on navy; small square yellow bullet markers; solid yellow bar anchoring the bottom edge of pages/slides; stage tracker "FORM → BUILT → CAPTURED". Data rule: yellow = revenue/gross figures, navy-blue = profit/net figures. Imagery: real towers and sites (night, coastal city), physical-to-digital transformation shots (building dematerialising into cubes/particles), raw concrete contrasted with warm finished interiors. Avoid: generic house/roof icons, circuit-board brains, AI spark icons, blockchain/network clichés, sci-fi HUDs, blue-purple AI gradients, crypto aesthetics.
